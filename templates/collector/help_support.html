Collector Help & Support

Field operational guidance and policies for FiCol field collectors.

Important Operational Policies

  • No handling of cash or payments: Collectors must not receive customer money under any circumstances. You may only assist customers with verified payment channels and submit payment proof for Back Office review.
  • Administrative review & approvals: Collectors cannot approve their own activity, payment verification, account closure, points, commissions, or assignment release requests. All verifications and decisions are strictly performed by Back Office administrators.
  • Internal records only: Logging Call, SMS, or Email contact attempts creates an internal audit record only; it never sends an external call, SMS, or email message.

1. Accepting Account Offers

Go to Account Offers and toggle your availability to Online. The platform discovers nearby accounts matching your approved client access within your service radius.

  • Review the approximate area, distance, and earnings terms before accepting.
  • You can accept up to 10 offers per rolling 24-hour period.
  • Once accepted, the account moves immediately to My Accounts.

2. Using My Accounts

Visit My Accounts to review all currently assigned accounts.

  • Your assigned accounts remain yours even when you toggle availability offline.
  • Each account card shows the customer name, reference number, target address, and action deadlines.
  • Select "Open account" to access full customer details, contact attempt history, visit tools, and notes.

3. Logging Contact Attempts

On the account detail page, use the Contact Attempt section above Collection Visits to log remote outreach.

  • Select the method (Call, SMS, or Email), outcome, and enter clear notes.
  • Internal record only: Saving an attempt records an internal history entry on the account. FiCol never initiates phone calls, sends text messages, or delivers emails to the customer.
  • Recent contact attempt history is displayed newest-first, separate from field visit records.

4. Field Collection Visits

When arriving at a customer location, use the Collection Visit section on the account page.

  • Start the visit on site. Only one ongoing visit is permitted across all your assigned accounts.
  • Capture your GPS location and record the outcome and detailed field observations.
  • Completed visits are submitted to Back Office for review.

5. Reporting Customer Payments

When a customer makes a direct payment or bank transfer:

  • Do not receive money: Field collectors are never permitted to take customer cash, checks, or transfers directly.
  • Guide the customer to pay directly through client-approved payment channels.
  • Submit a payment assistance report with the amount, reference, and photo proof (e.g. deposit slip or bank receipt) for independent Back Office verification.

6. Proposing Account Updates

If you discover new customer information during skip-tracing or field visits:

  • Submit an information proposal for new addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, contact persons, or payment arrangements.
  • Back Office reviews and validates the proposal before the official account records are updated.

7. Follow-ups & Scheduling

When an account requires a return visit or scheduled phone follow-up:

  • Submit a follow-up request with your suggested date and reason.
  • Back Office reviews the request and assigns an authorized start window and deadline.
  • Track upcoming and overdue deadlines on the Follow-ups page.

8. Requesting an Assignment Release

If an account cannot be serviced due to address invalidity, customer relocation, or security concerns:

  • Submit a release request from the account detail page with supporting explanation.
  • The account remains assigned to you until Back Office reviews and approves the release.

Need Administrative Assistance?

For changes to your collector profile, approval status, service area, client access permissions, account reassignments, or platform technical issues, please contact your FiCol administrator.
