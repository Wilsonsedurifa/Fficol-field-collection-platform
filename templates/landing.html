HOW IT WORKS

A practical workflow, stage by stage.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Eligible accounts are offered using verified location, active access, matching rules, and current ownership.

  2. 02

    Own

    Accepted work receives a clear owner, with guardrails around capacity and active visits.

  3. 03

    Act

    Collectors document visits, request follow-ups, report payments, and propose information updates.

  4. 04

    Review

    Authorized Back Office staff review work independently and make controlled operational decisions.
